# Mike's Math Coach Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026
**Developer:** Ain't Cloud Software

Mike's Math Coach provides offline mathematics tutoring. The current Android application stores practice results, mastery progress, preferences, and review schedules on the user's device. The shipped application does not request internet access and does not contain a remote account or analytics integration in the reviewed source.

## Information stored locally
The app can save answers and correctness, lesson progress, time spent practicing, review schedules, preference settings, and Equation Lab history. These records are stored locally on the device to personalize learning, and are not transmitted by the app to a developer server.

## Data sharing
The reviewed version does not send student learning records to the developer or third-party analytics services. Android's on-device text-to-speech service may process speech according to the user's device and installed speech engine settings; the developer does not receive speech data.

## Retention and deletion
Practice history remains on the device unless the user clears app storage or uninstalls the app. Android automatic app backup is disabled. Users should assume locally stored progress can be lost when the app is uninstalled, storage is cleared, or the device is replaced.

## Children's use
The app provides educational mathematics content and does not require account registration. Parents and guardians should review device-level controls and installed speech-engine privacy settings.

## Changes
This policy should be reviewed and updated whenever the app's data handling, third-party integrations, or account features change.

## Contact
**A verified developer support address must be published before this policy is used on Google Play.** Contact details have deliberately not been invented.

This document describes the current reviewed offline implementation. It must be checked against the final signed release and the actual Google Play Data safety disclosures.
